Service Tips, Tricks & Best Practices

Master your home and vehicle maintenance with certified pro tips. Essential 5 Do's & 5 Don'ts for every service to save costs, avoid breakdowns, and stay 100% safe.

On-Demand Chauffeur

Acting Driver Services

Professional guidelines for city errands, outstation family highway trips, and night chauffeur services in your personal car.

Book Verified Driver
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • 2-Minute Walkaround: Always check tyre pressure, fuel level, and Fastag balance with the driver before starting the trip.
  • Transmission Brief: Inform the driver about AMT/automatic transmission quirks, cruise control, or electronic handbrake operation beforehand.
  • Fatigue Management: For trips over 300 km or 6 hours, plan mandatory 15-minute tea breaks every 2.5 hours to keep driver alertness peak.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Verify Original DL: Inspect driver's physical Driving License and RTO badge upon arrival.
  • 2
    Keep Documents in Glovebox: Ensure RC book, valid vehicle insurance, and PUC certificate are readily accessible.
  • 3
    Test Brakes & Reverse Camera: Have the driver perform a gentle test brake and mirror alignment before entering busy roads.
  • 4
    Transparent Bata Terms: Settle night allowance and food bata terms clearly in advance as listed in the app.
  • 5
    Inspect Spare Wheel: Confirm the stepney tyre is inflated and the car jack/spanner is present in the boot.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Don't Encourage Over-speeding: Never ask the driver to violate speed limits or bypass red lights even if running late.
  • 2
    Don't Allow Handheld Phone Usage: Prohibit personal phone calls without a hands-free bluetooth setup while cruising.
  • 3
    Don't Leave Valuables in Cabin: Avoid leaving gold jewellery, open laptop bags, or high cash unattended inside the car.
  • 4
    Don't Force Continuous Midnight Drives: Never force an exhausted driver to continue driving past 1:00 AM without rest.
  • 5
    Don't Hand Keys Without Seatbelt: Never begin the ride until all occupants, including driver and co-passengers, are buckled up.
Two-Wheeler Care

Bike Mechanic & Maintenance

Crucial maintenance advice for scooters, commuter motorcycles, Royal Enfields, and electric two-wheelers.

Book Bike Mechanic
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • Engine Oil Dipstick Test: Inspect oil color every 1,000 km; if it turns charcoal black or feels gritty between fingers, schedule an immediate oil change.
  • Chain Slack Formula: Keep 25-30 mm of vertical chain play; an overtightened chain destroys the output shaft bearing and strains the gearbox.
  • Monsoon Waterproofing: Dab petroleum jelly or WD-40 on spark plug caps and ignition key slots to stop water-logged short circuits.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Maintain Cold Tyre Pressure: Check tyre PSI every week; correct pressure prevents rim dents and boosts mileage by 12%.
  • 2
    Lube Drive Chain Every 500 km: Clean with kerosene/chain cleaner and spray dedicated chain wax, especially after heavy rain.
  • 3
    Flush Brake Fluid Biannually: Replace DOT 3/4 brake fluid every 2 years to prevent brake lever sponginess and lockups.
  • 4
    Clean Air Filter Regularly: Tap out or replace the air filter every 4,000 km to protect engine piston rings from abrasive dust.
  • 5
    Insist on Genuine OEM Spares: Ensure oil filters, clutch cables, and spark plugs carry manufacturer hologram seals.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Don't Ride with Half-Clutch: Resting your fingers on the clutch lever glazes and burns friction plates within 6 months.
  • 2
    Don't Jet-Wash Electronics: Never blast high-pressure water jets directly into switchgear, digital consoles, or carburetors.
  • 3
    Don't Drag the Rear Brake: Resting your foot on the rear pedal overheats brake shoes, warps drum hubs, and drags engine power.
  • 4
    Don't Ignore Handlebar Wobbles: Front-end vibration signals worn steering cone bearings or bent alloy wheels.
  • 5
    Don't Overload Electricals: Avoid heavy high-watt auxiliary lights without an upgraded relay and wiring harness.
Automotive Specialist

Car Mechanic & Periodic Service

Professional car diagnostics, engine longevity practices, brake maintenance, and roadside emergency steps.

Book Car Mechanic
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • 30-Second Turbo Idle: Let turbocharged engines idle for 30 seconds before shutoff; this cools turbine bearings and prevents oil coking.
  • Never Mix Tap Water with Coolant: Tap water causes mineral scaling inside the radiator. Always use pre-mixed 50:50 glycol coolant or distilled water.
  • OBD-II Fault Log: If the Check Engine Light stays on, connect an OBD-II scanner immediately rather than resetting it blind.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Follow 10,000 km Oil Schedule: Replace engine oil, oil filter, and air filter strictly at scheduled mileage intervals.
  • 2
    Rotate Tyres Every 8,000 km: Perform 4-wheel rotation, computerized wheel alignment, and wheel balancing for even wear.
  • 3
    Check Brake Pad Thickness: Replace brake pads before the friction lining wears below 3 mm to protect expensive rotors.
  • 4
    Inspect Battery Terminal Voltage: Ensure the alternator generates 13.8V - 14.4V with headlights and AC turned on.
  • 5
    Replace Cabin AC Pollen Filter: Change the AC cabin filter every 6 months to ensure fresh odor-free airflow and maximum cooling.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Don't Open Hot Radiator Caps: Pressurized boiling coolant steam will burst out causing severe chemical burn injuries.
  • 2
    Don't Drive Through Deep Floods: Water sucked into the engine intake manifold causes instant catastrophic hydrostatic engine lock.
  • 3
    Don't Rest Hand on Gear Shifter: Continuous pressure on the gear stick prematurely wears synchronizer rings and shifter forks.
  • 4
    Don't Run on Empty Fuel Tank: Running with fuel light on sucks sediment from the tank bottom and overheats the in-tank fuel pump.
  • 5
    Don't Mix Oil Grades: Never mix synthetic 5W-30 with heavy mineral 20W-50 oil as viscosity breakdown harms hydraulic tappets.
Green Energy System

Solar Panel & Inverter Maintenance

Proven ways to maintain maximum PV generation efficiency, inverter battery health, and lightning surge safety.

Book Solar Technician
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • Wash Before 8 AM: Cold water splashed onto solar panels heated by the afternoon sun causes micro-fractures in tempered solar glass.
  • Spot Bird Droppings Fast: Dried bird droppings block light and create localized hot-spots that permanently burn solar cell wafer strings.
  • Generation Logging: Check weekly unit generation (kWh) on your solar app; a sudden 15% drop points to shading or loose MC4 connectors.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Wash Twice Monthly with Soft Mop: Use soft microfiber mops and filtered demineralized water to remove pollution film.
  • 2
    Annual Structure Bolt Torque Check: Tighten all aluminum rail clamps and anchor bolts to withstand coastal gale winds.
  • 3
    Verify Earthing Pit Value (< 5 Ohms): Water your chemical earthing pit every summer to keep earth resistance low against lightning.
  • 4
    Keep Inverter Well-Ventilated: Ensure 1 foot clearance around inverter cooling fins and clean fan vents from spider webs.
  • 5
    Trim Tree Branches: Prune nearby trees before monsoon to prevent shadow casting from morning to evening.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Don't Use Abrasive Scrubbers: Metal scrubbers, harsh detergents, or scouring powders strip the anti-reflective glass coating.
  • 2
    Don't Walk on Solar Panels: Never step or place heavy tools on panels; invisible cell micro-cracks drastically reduce lifespan.
  • 3
    Don't Clean Under Blazing Midday Sun: High thermal shock can crack glass panels and water evaporates too fast leaving hard stains.
  • 4
    Don't Touch DC Connectors Wet: High DC voltage (300V - 600V) from solar arrays is lethal; never tamper with cables with bare hands.
  • 5
    Don't Bypass SPD Protections: Never disconnect DC/AC surge protection devices (SPD) or fuses in the distribution box.
Climate Control

Air Conditioner Care & Gas Safety

Save up to 25% on summer electricity bills, eliminate mold smells, and protect your inverter compressor.

Book AC Technician
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • The 24°C Comfort Standard: Running your AC at 24°C instead of 18°C saves 6% electricity per degree (nearly 24% lower power bill!).
  • Pair with Ceiling Fan at Low Speed: A low-speed ceiling fan circulates cool air evenly, allowing you to raise AC temperature by 2°C with identical comfort.
  • Dry Mode for Rainy Seasons: Switch to 'Dry' mode (water droplet icon) during monsoon; it strips humidity without freezing the room.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Wash Nylon Mesh Filters Every 15 Days: Rinse dust filters with plain tap water and air-dry before popping them back.
  • 2
    Annual High-Pressure Jet Chemical Wash: Deep clean indoor evaporator and outdoor condenser coils before summer peaks.
  • 3
    Maintain 2 Feet Clearance for Outdoor Unit: Keep the condenser fan clear of drying clothes, walls, and potted plants.
  • 4
    Inspect Drain Pipe Slope: Verify the drain hose tilts downward smoothly so condensation water never overflows inside.
  • 5
    Seal Window & Door Crevices: Install rubber draft seals along room doors to prevent chilled air escaping.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Don't Run with Doors Ajar: Leaving bedroom doors open overworks the compressor continuously, leading to early motor burnout.
  • 2
    Don't Place Lamps Near Thermostat: Heat from lamps or televisions tricks the temperature sensor into cooling endlessly.
  • 3
    Don't Ignore Indoor Water Dripping: Condensate leakage breeds black mold inside wall plaster and ruins bedroom wallpaper.
  • 4
    Don't Ignore Ice on Copper Pipes: Frosting indicates a dangerous gas leak or severely clogged airflow; turn off AC immediately.
  • 5
    Don't Use Underrated Extension Plugs: Never run ACs on generic 6A outlets without dedicated 16A MCB wiring.
Safety & Wiring

Home Electrical Safety & MCB Care

Protect your family from shocks, prevent electrical short circuits, and safeguard expensive gadgets.

Book Certified Electrician
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • Monthly RCCB Test: Press the 'T' (Test) push button on your main electrical board RCCB once a month; it must instantly trip to prove shock protection works.
  • The Warm Plug Warning: If any 3-pin plug feels hot to the touch after 20 minutes, the socket internal copper leaves are loose and sparking. Replace it immediately.
  • Surge Protector Strip: Always plug OLED TVs, desktop PCs, and gaming consoles into spike-guard surge protectors rather than bare wall sockets.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Cut Main MCB Before Repairs: Always isolate the main electrical breaker before changing light fixtures or ceiling fans.
  • 2
    Use ISI Fire-Retardant (FR) Copper Wires: Ensure 4.0 sq.mm wiring for AC/geysers and 2.5 sq.mm for standard power points.
  • 3
    Verify Active Earth Resistance: Ensure proper 3-pin grounding for metallic body appliances (fridges, microwaves, geysers).
  • 4
    Upgrade to Modular Switches: Replace old toggle switches with arc-shielded modular safety switchboards.
  • 5
    Unplug During Severe Thunderstorms: Disconnect Wi-Fi routers and cable TV lines to guard against lightning voltage spikes.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Don't Daisy-Chain Multi-Plugs: Never stack multiple high-watt heaters or irons into one single extension socket.
  • 2
    Don't Touch Switches with Wet Hands: Water drastically lowers skin electrical resistance, inviting fatal 230V shocks.
  • 3
    Don't Wrap Thick Copper in Fuse: Bypassing fuse ratings with thick copper wire causes wall conduits to catch fire.
  • 4
    Don't Run Cords Under Carpets: Foot traffic frays internal wire insulation unseen, causing sudden floor fires.
  • 5
    Don't Ignore Burning Plastic Odor: A fishy/burning smell indicates overheating wiring. Turn off the main switch immediately!
Pipeline & Drainage

Plumbing Care & Water Leak Prevention

Prevent water damage, unclog stubborn drainage traps, and double the lifespan of taps and geysers.

Book Expert Plumber
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • The Toilet Food Color Test: Put 5 drops of food dye in the flush cistern; if color creeps into the bowl within 15 minutes without flushing, your flapper valve is silently wasting 150 liters daily!
  • Vinegar Aerator Soak: Unscrew low-flow tap aerators once every 3 months and soak in white vinegar for 30 minutes to dissolve mineral calcium crust.
  • Main Stopcock Location: Make sure every adult at home knows the exact position of the main water supply valve to shut it off during burst pipe emergencies.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Use Stainless Steel Sink Strainers: Catch food leftovers, tea leaves, and bathroom hair before they enter drain pipes.
  • 2
    Clean Overhead Water Tank Biannually: Wash sediment silt and algae buildup twice a year to maintain clean drinking water.
  • 3
    Wrap Teflon Tape Clockwise: Wrap 5-6 layers of PTFE Teflon tape clockwise over threads before screwing on new brass bib taps.
  • 4
    Test Geyser Pressure Relief Valve: Pull the spring safety valve lever on the water heater yearly to prevent tank burst risks.
  • 5
    Replace Aging Tap Washers Promptly: Fix dripping taps early before the constant drip cuts a groove into the brass valve seat.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Don't Pour Hot Cooking Oil Down Sinks: Cooking fats solidify into rock-hard 'fatbergs' inside drain pipes that cause nasty backflows.
  • 2
    Don't Flush Wipes or Dental Floss: Wet wipes and sanitary items do not dissolve; they clump and block city sewer lines.
  • 3
    Don't Overtighten Tap Handles: Forcing tap handles beyond their stop point crushes rubber seals and cracks ceramic discs.
  • 4
    Don't Overuse Caustic Drain Acids: Repeated harsh acid uncloggers generate heat that softens PVC joints and rots metal traps.
  • 5
    Don't Ignore Damp Yellow Wall Patches: Wall dampness indicates concealed pipe leaks that slowly weaken concrete foundations.
Woodcraft & Hardware

Carpentry & Wooden Furniture Care

Preserve solid teak and modular plywood furniture, fix sticking doors, and silence squeaky hinges.

Book Skilled Carpenter
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • Monsoon Door Sticking Fix: When wooden doors swell during rain, rub a candle or dry bar of soap along the rubbing latch edge rather than shaving wood away.
  • Loose Screw Matchstick Trick: If a hinge screw spins loose in stripped wood, insert two matchsticks dipped in Fevicol wood glue, break flush, and re-drive the screw tightly.
  • Walnut Scratch Eraser: For minor scratches on polished wood, rub a natural walnut kernel over the scratch; natural oils instantly blend the blemish.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Lube Hinges & Drawer Slides with WD-40: Spray ball-bearing drawer channels and door hinges every 6 months.
  • 2
    Use Felt Floor Protectors: Stick adhesive felt pads under wooden chair and table legs to stop floor scratches.
  • 3
    Buff with Natural Beeswax: Apply wood polish or beeswax once a year to nourish dry wood grain and seal against humidity.
  • 4
    Choose 304 Stainless Steel Kitchen Hinges: Ensure modular kitchen hydraulic hinges are rust-proof marine-grade SS304.
  • 5
    Wipe Spills Immediately: Blot water or coffee spills right away to avoid ring watermarks and veneer blistering.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Don't Place Hot Cookware on Wood: Hot pans scorch lacquer coats leaving permanent opaque white burn rings.
  • 2
    Don't Place Furniture in Direct Sun: Harsh sunlight fades stain finishes, cracks natural timber, and dries out joints.
  • 3
    Don't Drag Heavy Sofas & Wardrobes: Always lift furniture with two people to prevent snapping bottom leg tenons.
  • 4
    Don't Overload Modular Drawers: Respect maximum load ratings (usually 25 kg) to prevent bending telescopic rails.
  • 5
    Don't Clean with Acidic Sprays: Avoid harsh glass/bathroom cleaners that strip natural polyurethane wood coatings.
Hygiene & Sanitization

Home Deep Cleaning & Sanitization

Effortless top-to-bottom cleaning workflows, kitchen degreasing hacks, and allergy-free living.

Book Deep Cleaning
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • Top-to-Bottom Rule: Dust ceiling fans and top curtain pelmets first; falling debris can be vacuumed off floors at the final step without double work.
  • Microwave Steam Clean Hack: Microwave a cup of water with lemon slices for 3 minutes; the steam softens baked-on oil and food splatters for an effortless wipe.
  • Color-Coded Microfiber: Dedicate Blue for glass/mirrors, Green for kitchen counters, and Red/Yellow for bathroom sanitary fixtures.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Vacuum Mattresses & Sofas Fortnightly: Pull out allergen dust mites from fabric seams with a high-suction vacuum.
  • 2
    Boil Kitchen Chimney Filters Monthly: Soak stainless steel mesh filters in boiling water with soda ash to melt stubborn grease.
  • 3
    Run Bathroom Exhaust 20 Mins Post Shower: Clear damp humidity to stop black mildew growing inside tile grout joints.
  • 4
    Sanitize High-Touch Handles: Wipe door knobs, fridge handles, light switches, and TV remotes with alcohol disinfectant.
  • 5
    Deep Clean Pre-Festivals: Schedule a mechanized floor scrubbing and sofa shampooing service twice a year.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Never Mix Bleach and Acids: Combining chlorine bleach with toilet acid or ammonia releases deadly toxic chloramine gas!
  • 2
    Don't Use Acid on Italian Marble: Hydrochloric acid dissolves calcium in marble and granite, etching permanent dull scars.
  • 3
    Don't Scrub Glass with Steel Wool: Metal wire scrubbers leave irreversible swirls and scratches across glass cooktops.
  • 4
    Don't Store Wet Mops in Buckets: Soggy mops develop foul mildew odor and spread bacterial colonies across clean floors.
  • 5
    Don't Skip Dusting Before Mopping: Mopping directly over loose dust creates muddy streaks that soil floor grout.
Wall Decor & Protection

Painting & Waterproofing Tips

Achieve flawless streak-free wall finishes, fix efflorescence blisters, and make paint last 7+ years.

Book Painting Pro
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • The 80% Prep Rule: A long-lasting paint finish is 80% scraping, sanding, and primer bonding, and only 20% roller rolling.
  • Moisture Meter Check: Never apply putty or paint on walls with > 12% moisture content; trapped moisture guarantees peeling bubbles.
  • Save Touch-up Paint in Mason Jars: Decant 250ml of leftover wall paint into sealed glass jars labeled by room for seamless future scuff touch-ups.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Apply Alkali-Resistant Primer: Always apply a quality primer coat over new plaster or putty before topcoat emulsion.
  • 2
    Apply Painter's Masking Tape: Tape along skirting tiles, switchboards, and door trims to ensure razor-sharp cut lines.
  • 3
    Allow 4-6 Hours Between Coats: Ensure the first coat cures fully before rolling the second coat to prevent roller marks.
  • 4
    Sand with 180-Grit Paper: Gently smooth dried putty layers to eliminate ridges before roller painting.
  • 5
    Pick Low-VOC Anti-Bacterial Paint: Choose non-toxic low-odor paint for children's bedrooms and elderly living spaces.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Don't Paint During Heavy Rains: High humidity prevents acrylic water-based paint from curing and invites fungal mold.
  • 2
    Don't Peel Tape After Full Curing: Pull masking tape at a 45° angle while the final coat is still slightly tacky to avoid paint tear.
  • 3
    Don't Paint Over White Salt (Efflorescence): Treat rising dampness and salt deposit chemistry before applying fresh paint.
  • 4
    Don't Dilute Beyond 40% Water: Over-thinning paint with excess water reduces opacity, scrub resistance, and color depth.
  • 5
    Don't Pour Paint Wash Down Drains: Residue latex solidifies in domestic traps; let paint dry on rags and dispose in solid waste.
Hardware & Security

Computer, Laptop & Data Security Tips

Speed up sluggish laptops, prevent motherboard chip overheating, and safeguard valuable photos and files.

Book PC Technician
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • SSD Transformation: Upgrading an older laptop from a mechanical hard drive (HDD) to an NVMe/SATA SSD makes it boot in 10 seconds and feel 5x faster for just ₹1,500.
  • The 3-2-1 Backup Rule: Keep 3 copies of your crucial data, on 2 different physical drives, with 1 copy securely uploaded to encrypted cloud storage.
  • Annual Thermal Paste Renewal: If the laptop fan roars constantly, renewing dry thermal paste on the CPU drops operating temperatures by up to 15°C.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Elevate Laptop on Flat Surface: Keep the bottom air intake grille elevated on a ventilated stand to ensure cooling airflow.
  • 2
    Keep Battery Between 20% & 80%: Avoid continuous 100% plug-in trickle charging to extend lithium-ion battery life.
  • 3
    Install Weekly Security Updates: Patch Windows / macOS updates regularly to defend against zero-day ransomware.
  • 4
    Clean Keyboard with Compressed Air: Blow out trapped biscuit crumbs and dust particles before they stick scissor switches.
  • 5
    Use a Grounded Surge Protector: Shield desktop computers and external drives from lightning spikes and voltage surges.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Never Use Laptops on Beds/Blankets: Soft fabric suffocates intake vents and draws blanket lint into the cooling fan.
  • 2
    Don't Drink Near Keyboards: Coffee and water spills cause instantaneous electrolytic motherboard short-circuits.
  • 3
    Don't Download Cracked Software: Torrents and pirated game cracks are the #1 source of keystroke loggers and trojans.
  • 4
    Don't Force Stuck USB/HDMI Ports: Forcing inverted USB connectors fractures the internal pins and short-circuits the motherboard.
  • 5
    Don't Lift by Screen Corners: Gripping a laptop by the LCD panel corner twists the display matrix and snaps hinge anchors.

Hygienic skin care practices, allergy test guidelines, post-wax care, and radiant bridal preparations.

Safe Pest Management

Pest Control & Termite Prevention

Keep your home free of cockroaches, bed bugs, termites, and rodents with certified safe treatments.

Book Pest Control
Pro Tips & Tricks:
  • Dot-Sized Gel Placement: Cockroach bait gel works best when placed in tiny pea-sized dots inside dark cabinet hinge corners, not smeared in open areas.
  • Cardboard Box Warning: Never hoard damp delivery cardboard boxes in lofts; the starch glue inside corrugated cardboard is cockroaches' favorite breeding feast.
  • Boric Acid Behind Fridge: A light dusting of boric acid powder in the warm zone behind refrigerator compressor eradicates stubborn roach colonies naturally.
Top 5 Do's
  • 1
    Empty & Cover Kitchen Utensils: Protect plates, spices, and open food under plastic sheets before any chemical spraying.
  • 2
    Vacate Premises for 3 Hours: Keep children, pregnant women, and pets away during and for 3 hours after treatment.
  • 3
    Seal Pipe Wall Holes with Steel Wool: Pack stainless steel wool around plumbing pipe gaps to stop rodents chewing inside.
  • 4
    Ventilate for 30 Mins Before Re-entry: Open all windows and run exhaust fans to circulate fresh air before settling in.
  • 5
    Wipe Food Prep Counters Post Treatment: Wash countertops with warm soapy water before cooking the next family meal.
Top 5 Don'ts
  • 1
    Don't Mop Wall Skirting for 48 Hours: Washing sprayed wall baseboards washes away the active chemical barrier.
  • 2
    Don't Leave Pet Food Bowls Overnight: Leftover pet kibble and unsealed water bowls attract nocturnal rats and roaches.
  • 3
    Don't Store Damp Timber Near Walls: Stacking wet firewood or untreated scrap wood against walls invites subterranean termites.
  • 4
    Don't Spray Aerosols on Bedbugs: Over-the-counter bug sprays irritate bedbugs, scattering them deeper into mattress seams.
  • 5
    Don't Ignore Mud Tubes on Walls: Thin brown mud tunnels along door frames are proof of an active termite colony destroying wood.
